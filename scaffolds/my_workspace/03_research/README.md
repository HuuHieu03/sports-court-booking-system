# Technical Research (`03_research/`)

## Purpose
Document structured investigations into competing architectural solutions, ORM alternatives, concurrency control mechanisms, and benchmarking strategies.

## Research vs Official Decisions
- Research explores trade-offs and presents evidence.
- A research note is NOT an official architectural decision until reviewed by the team and accepted as an ADR in `documentation/03_architecture/`.
